Aviation medicals

Where can I get a SACAA Class 2 medical in Midrand?

+

Dr A.O. Ndhlovu is a SACAA Regular Designated Aviation Medical Examiner (DAME), number MR 336, at Clinotrend Health & Aviation, Main Terminal Building, Gate 2, Office TG6B, Grand Central Airport, 3 New Road, Midrand, 1685. He examines applicants for Class II and Class IV medical certificates. Book online, call 011 568 7979 or send a WhatsApp to 078 255 1405.

Which aviation medicals does Dr Ndhlovu do?

+

Dr A.O. Ndhlovu is listed by the South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA) as a Regular Designated Aviation Medical Examiner, number MR 336. SACAA authorises Regular DAMEs to examine applicants for and issue Class II and Class IV medical certificates.

Who needs a Class II medical?

+

SACAA lists private pilots and student (pupil) pilots under Class II. SACAA's aviation medicine page also lists cabin crew under Class II.

Who needs a Class IV medical?

+

SACAA lists recreational pilots under Class IV, for example microlight, glider, gyroplane and free balloon pilots.

Which class do cabin crew need?

+

SACAA's aviation medicine page lists cabin crew under Class II, and SACAA's frequently asked questions state that since May 2019 cabin crew require a Class 4 medical certificate. Both classes fall within a Regular DAME's scope. Tell us you are cabin crew when you book and we will confirm which certificate applies to you.

Can I get a Class I or Class III medical here?

+

No. Class I (airline transport and commercial pilots) and Class III (air traffic controllers) medicals can only be done by a SACAA Senior DAME. Dr Ndhlovu is a Regular DAME, so these classes are not offered at this practice.

What should I bring to my aviation medical?

+

Bring your ID or passport, any aviation licence you hold, your most recent aviation medical certificate if you have one, your pilot logbook if you are renewing, your medical aid card if applicable, a list of your medicines, and the glasses, contact lenses or hearing aids you use. If you have a medical condition, bring recent reports from your doctor or specialist. SACAA regulations require you to show proof of identity, your licence and your latest medical certificate, give your medical history and sign a declaration.

Which tests are done during the aviation medical?

+

A resting ECG, vision testing, hearing testing (audiometry) and urine screening are done on site, together with your medical history and a physical examination.

How long does an aviation medical take?

+

Allow about 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the service and on whether you bring all your documents. Please arrive 10 minutes early to complete paperwork.

Will I get my certificate on the same day?

+

If you meet the medical standards and no further reports are needed, the examiner can issue your certificate at the end of the examination. Some applicants need extra reports or a SACAA review before a certificate can be issued. In that case we will tell you what is needed.

How long is my medical certificate valid?

+

According to SACAA, a Class 2 certificate is valid for 24 months if you are under 40 and 12 months if you are 40 or older. A Class 4 certificate is valid for 60 months if you are under 40 and 36 months if you are 40 or older. Your examiner will confirm the validity that applies to you, as some medical conditions need more frequent review.

How much does an aviation medical cost?

+

Fees are not published on the website. We tell you the fee when you book. Call 011 568 7979 or send a WhatsApp to 078 255 1405 to ask.

Can I fly in for my medical?

+

Yes. Grand Central Airport is FAGC. The apron and visitor aircraft parking are directly outside the Gate 2 terminal doors, and the practice is in the Main Terminal Building at Gate 2.

Occupational health

Which occupational health medicals do you offer?

+

Pre-employment, periodic and exit medicals, PrDP (professional driving permit) medical examinations, workplace health risk assessments and incapacity consultations. Audiometry (hearing) and lung function (spirometry) tests are available.

Do you work with employers?

+

Yes. We offer occupational health services for businesses, including pre-employment screenings, wellness days and ongoing employee health programmes. Contact us on 011 568 7979 or info@clinotrend-health.co.za for a corporate quote.

Who does the occupational health medical?

+

Dr A.O. Ndhlovu, who holds a Diploma in Occupational Health (DOHM, University of Pretoria) in addition to his MBChB (UCT).

What should employees bring?

+

Their ID or passport, any forms or job requirements from the employer, glasses or contact lenses if they use them, and a list of their current medicines.

How much does an occupational medical cost?

+

Fees depend on the tests the employer needs, so they are not published on the website. Ask us for a quote.

GP consultations

Is there a GP at Grand Central Airport in Midrand?

+

Yes. Dr A.O. Ndhlovu offers general practice (GP) consultations at Clinotrend Health & Aviation, Main Terminal Building, Gate 2, Office TG6B, Grand Central Airport, 3 New Road, Midrand, 1685.

What can I see the GP for?

+

Acute illness in adults and children, chronic condition care (for example diabetes, high blood pressure, asthma and HIV), blood tests, ECG and general health screening, and referrals to specialists when needed.

Do you accept medical aid?

+

Medical aid cover differs between services and plans. Bring your medical aid card and check with us before your visit on 011 568 7979. Self-pay patients receive a quote before treatment.

How do I book a GP appointment?

+

Book online, call 011 568 7979 or send a WhatsApp to 078 255 1405. Online booking is not for emergencies. In an emergency call 10177, or 112 from a mobile phone.
